AnonyNotes

Hard thoughts to share? Want it out of your mind without telling anyone specifically?


Welcome to AnonyNotes, an anonymous message board. Think of it like the online version of the underpass littered with messages from random strangers. Yell your frustration, confess your secrets, scribble nonsense, and whatever else you so desire. You can set it to last as long as you wish, whether 2 hours, a day, or however long this site stays up.


AnonyNotes does not collect or retain any information aside from the submitted text, and for no longer than the specified time. All information in AnonyNotes is publicly available information. Practice internet safety and do not post identifiable information.

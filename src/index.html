Torrey Pines Gliderport

Torrey Pines Gliderport is situated upon the cliffs of La Jolla overlooking Blacks Beach. I love going close to cliff edge with the ocean breeze in my face and the sounds of the waves.

An ocean view overlooking the horizon from the cliff's edge. The sky is cloudy, with small breaks of sunlight scattered amongst the clouds. The waves below are dotted with flickering reflected lights upon the ocean surface.

Travel Tips

I highly recommend going during sunset or sunrise when the skies are clear. It is a beautiful, peaceful view. Great for meditation or just plain relaxation. If you mind getting sand on your things, then I also recommend bringing a blanket to lay down on.

Nearby locations you should visit in the area:

  • Ho Chi Minh trailhead: If you are more of a hiker, you can check out this trail. Be warned, it is known as a difficult trail, with steep drops and sandy surfaces.
  • UCSD Scripps Pier: The pier used by the Scripps Institute of Oceanography. A stellar nighttime view if you manage to visit when the bioluminescent algae glow with the waves. If coming from Gliderport, you may need to ride the 101 bus bound for UTC at N Torrey Pines Road, transfer at the La Jolla Playhouse onto the 30 bound for Old Town, and get off at Naga Way.
  • La Jolla Tide Pools: The La Jolla Tide Pools expose a lot of rock and little ocean crawlies during low tide, so if you're into that, you're in for a treat! Aside from that, the walk is relaxing with the ocean breeze and sound of the waves. If traveling from Gliderport, you can take the 101 bus bound for UTC at N Torrey Pines Road, transfer at the La Jolla Playhouse onto the 30 bus bound for Old Town, and get off at Pearl St & Cuvier. Then you'll walk down Pearl St down, take a right at Olivetas Ave, and left at Coast Blvd until you see the coast.

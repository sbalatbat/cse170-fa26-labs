Tamagoyaki

Tools & Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • A tamagoyaki pan (or a simple frying pan)
  • Spatula
  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Crack the 2 eggs open into a bowl and beat them until the egg yolks are fully incorporated.
  2. Scoop 2 1/2 teaspoons of sugar and 2 teaspoons of sugar into the eggs and mix until the mixture looks sufficiently brown.
  3. Spray or coat the surface of the pan with oil. Heat the pan on medium heat. To check if it's hot enough, flick a droplet of water onto the pan and it should evaporate almost instantaneously.
  4. Once the pan is hot enough, pour a thin coat of the mixture onto the surface. I recommend using a measuring cup with a long spout, if you happen to have one.
  5. Cook until the bottom is solid and carefully roll up the egg on the pan. I usually end up rolling it as if I'm folding it in layers, but it's up you.
  6. Pour and cook until you've used up all the mixture.
  7. Enjoy!
